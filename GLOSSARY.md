# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, to get their money back for one purchase; our team reviews it by hand.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to the learner when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending**:
A Refund Request that has been raised and not yet reviewed.
_Avoid_: Requested, open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request our team has accepted; a Refund follows.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request our team has turned down; nothing is refunded.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Window**:
The period after a purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Chargeback**:
Not used for anything in Cadence. In payments it means a dispute the learner raises with their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence — never our own Refund Request process.
